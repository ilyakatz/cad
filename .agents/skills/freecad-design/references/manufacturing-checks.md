# Manufacturing checks

Read this reference when the requested design is intended for injection molding or machining.

## Injection molding

- Identify pull direction, parting line, shutoffs, and unavoidable undercuts.
- Keep nominal walls reasonably uniform; core out thick masses and use ribs instead.
- Add draft to pull-direction faces when tooling intent is established.
- Use fillets at wall and rib roots; avoid sharp internal corners.
- Keep ribs thinner than adjacent nominal walls to reduce sink.
- Check boss wall thickness, support, fastener specification, and assembly access.
- Separate nominal part geometry from resin shrink compensation.
- Confirm ejector-safe surfaces and cosmetic-face requirements before tooling release.

## Machining

- Confirm stock form, setups, datum scheme, tool access, and minimum internal radii.
- Avoid deep narrow pockets and inaccessible re-entrant geometry.
- Specify threads, counterbores, countersinks, and toleranced bores explicitly.
- Distinguish as-machined edges from required chamfers or fillets.
- Identify critical inspection dimensions and their datum references.

## Fits and tolerances

- Do not apply a general tolerance to sliding, press-fit, bearing, sealing, or snap interfaces.
- Define diametral versus radial clearance unambiguously.
- Include process capability and material/environment effects in functional fits.
- Treat photograph-derived dimensions as assumptions until measured.
